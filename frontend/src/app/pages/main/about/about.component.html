About Us

JobHuntiger is a simple and efficient application designed to help job seekers organize and track their job applications in one place. Instead of managing dozens of applications through spreadsheets, emails, or notes, JobHuntiger provides a centralized dashboard for monitoring your job search progress.

With JobHuntiger, you can record the companies you have applied to, keep track of job titles, store the resumes used for each application, and easily review your application history whenever needed.

The goal of JobHuntiger is to make job hunting more structured and less stressful. By keeping all application information organized, you can focus more on preparing for interviews and finding the right opportunities rather than searching through scattered documents and emails.

Key Features

  • Track job applications from a single dashboard
  • Search and filter applications by company or job title
  • Store and manage resumes associated with applications
  • View application history and important dates
  • Stay organized throughout your job search journey
